# Enterprise onboarding: from signed contract to first routed conversation

1. Kickoff within 5 working days of signature; CS owns it, Solutions joins.
2. Connect channels in this order: web chat (no third-party approval), then WhatsApp (needs
   Meta business verification, 2–10 days), then email.
3. CRM integration: Salesforce or HubSpot lookup by email/phone; case write-back is phase 2.
4. Routing rules: start with at most three queues. Every customer asks for twelve.
5. Human handoff tested end to end with a real agent before go-live.

**Expired 2026-08-18.** Step 2 is out of date: email now goes first for customers coming from
a helpdesk, and the template-approval step is missing (see [[OB-onboarding-guide-skips-template-approval]]).
