# Conversation

The unit an agent works on and, since July 2026, the unit some customers are billed on.
One conversation can contain many [[GL-session|sessions]]: a customer who writes on Monday and
again on Wednesday about the same order is one conversation, two sessions.

**Why this entry exists.** In the Beta Corp pricing negotiation, Sales said "conversation" and
meant sessions, and the quote was off by a factor of 2.4. Say "conversation" only for the
billable, closable unit.
