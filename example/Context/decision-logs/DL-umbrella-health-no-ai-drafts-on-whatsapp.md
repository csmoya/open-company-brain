# Umbrella Health: no AI-drafted replies on WhatsApp until the BAA is signed

**Decision (2026-05-06).** AI reply drafts are disabled for Umbrella Health's workspace, on every
channel, until our model provider's business associate agreement covers their region. Agents
use macros instead.

**Why it is opinionated.** Sales wanted to ship drafts "with a disclaimer". Maya refused: a
disclaimer does not make patient data leave the boundary any less. This is a hard no, not a
toggle the customer can flip.

**Reopen when.** Legal confirms BAA coverage in writing. Not before, not "soon".
