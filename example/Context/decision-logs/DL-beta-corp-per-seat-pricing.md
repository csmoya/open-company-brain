# Beta Corp stays on per-seat pricing for the 2026 renewal

**Decision (2026-02-10, Q1 business review).** Beta Corp renews for 2026 on the per-seat plan:
42 agent seats, conversation quota bundled at 3,000/month, overage at list price.

**Why.** Beta Corp's procurement only approves line items they can forecast, and seats are the
one number their finance team already budgets. Lena pushed for per-conversation pricing and lost
the argument in the room: "we know how many people we employ, we do not know how many customers
will write to us."

**What we gave up.** Beta Corp's volume is spiky (renewal season in insurance), so per-seat
leaves money on the table in Q4 and makes them feel overcharged in Q2.
