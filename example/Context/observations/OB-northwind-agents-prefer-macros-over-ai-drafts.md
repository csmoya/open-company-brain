# Northwind's agents prefer macros over AI drafts for order-status questions

From a shadowing session in September 2025, written up only in August 2026 while preparing
Northwind's QBR. For "where is my order" questions — most of their WhatsApp volume — agents
ignored the AI draft and fired a macro with the tracking link. Their words: the draft was
"polite and slow", the macro was "ugly and right".

Worth repeating with other high-volume retail accounts: offer macros first, AI drafts for the
long tail.
